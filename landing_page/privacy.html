Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At Zest, we take your campus privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect when you use the Zest mobile application and website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect basic academic registration data (name, student email, roll number, department) to generate event tickets, verify attendance, and issue certificates.

2. How We Use Information

Your information is solely used for college event administration, gate check-in verification, and leaderboards. We do not sell your personal data to third parties or advertisers.

3. Contact

Questions about your data? Reach out to privacy@zest.app.
